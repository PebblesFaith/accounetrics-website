# Purpose

Volume II establishes the enterprise color architecture governing the AccouNetrics ecosystem.

This volume treats color as a structured engineering and communication system. Every approved production color must support a documented purpose, preserve semantic meaning, meet accessibility requirements, and remain consistent across products, services, reports, dashboards, publications, and future platforms.

---

# Relationship to Volume I

Volume I — Design Philosophy established the principles governing clarity, consistency, accessibility, architectural thinking, engineering quality, trust, and enterprise experience.

Volume II applies those principles to color.

The requirements contained within this volume must remain consistent with:

- Human-Centered Engineering
- Architectural Thinking
- Engineering Principles
- Trust by Design
- Enterprise Experience Principles
- The AEDS Manifesto

---

# Volume Objectives

The objectives of Volume II are to:

- establish color as an enterprise communication system;
- define semantic color roles;
- separate semantic meaning from implementation values;
- establish accessibility and contrast requirements;
- define brand and neutral color systems;
- standardize enterprise status indicators;
- define security-related color communication;
- govern financial and analytical visualization;
- establish design-token implementation;
- define approval and change-control requirements.

---

# How to Read This Volume

Volume II progresses from conceptual foundations into implementation and governance.

Chapters 01 and 02 establish the language and philosophy of enterprise color.

Chapters 03 through 08 define semantic, accessibility, brand, operational, analytical, and security requirements.

Chapter 09 defines design-token and implementation expectations.

Chapter 10 establishes governance, review, versioning, and future-change requirements.
