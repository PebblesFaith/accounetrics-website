# Purpose

This document is reserved for the official publication summary of Volume II — Color Architecture.

The final summary will document the purpose of Volume II, the standards established by Chapters 01 through 10, the relationship between color semantics and implementation, accessibility and contrast requirements, brand, status, analytical, and security color architecture, design-token implementation, color governance, and the transition into Volume III — Background Architecture.

This document will not be treated as Chapter 11.
