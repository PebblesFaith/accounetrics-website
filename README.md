# Purpose

The AccouNetrics Enterprise Design System (AEDS) is the authoritative engineering, visual, accessibility, interaction, documentation, and governance standard for the AccouNetrics ecosystem.

This publication index provides controlled navigation across the complete AEDS manual, identifies the status of each volume, and establishes the relationship between foundational principles, implementation specifications, reusable components, and future platform expansion.

The AEDS is designed to support consistent decision-making across:

- Corporate websites
- Enterprise applications
- Accounting and financial systems
- Administrative portals
- Compliance interfaces
- Audit workflows
- Security-sensitive experiences
- Data visualization
- Reports and publications
- Responsive web interfaces
- Future mobile and desktop applications
- Internal engineering documentation

---

# Publication Authority

The AEDS governs the design and implementation of AccouNetrics digital products and related enterprise experiences.

Formal AEDS publications use:

- Controlled document identifiers
- Volume and chapter designations
- Version numbers
- Publication statuses
- Revision histories
- AEDS Publication Milestones
- Engineering review records
- Approval records
- Publication dates
- Rights notices
- Git-based version control

Approved publications remain authoritative unless superseded by a formally approved revision.

# Governance Statement

The AEDS is a controlled engineering publication.

Changes to approved standards must:

1. Preserve the established publication hierarchy.
2. Use the applicable document identifier.
3. Record the revision in the document Revision History.
4. update the applicable AEDS Publication Milestone when required.
5. receive engineering review and approval.
6. be documented through Git version control.
7. maintain consistency with Volume I — Design Philosophy.

Informal experimentation may occur within prototype directories, but experimental material does not become normative until formally reviewed and published within the manual.
